**Adversary AI tooling as a market, read from public reporting.**

The models running inside an agentic intrusion have no economics. The people buying the tooling do. Budget, reputation, trust, and vetting cost are all constraints, and constraints are readable.

This section is the constraint read applied one layer up from the agent, on the market that supplies it.

## Why this is the right layer

The standing claim from the case file work is that attribution tells measuring scarcity degrade in proportion to how unscarce the actor is. An optimizer with allocated compute and no consequences leaks almost nothing.

The market does not have that property. Someone decided what to build, what to charge, and who to sell to, under real constraints. The tells did not leave the world. They moved to a layer that is still legible.
